# Execution evidence

The portfolio is a VND simulation. Fractional adjusted shares and fractional lượng make the 10m VND opening capital investable across all sleeves. Actual HOSE board lots reached 100 shares on 4 January 2021. A close-price fraction cannot reproduce a board-lot or odd-lot fill. Equity brokerage of 10 bp per side is the GOLD–K5 comparison assumption. T+2 is modeled for the entire sample. Settlement availability and broker charges within each historical day have no supporting transaction receipts. [HOSE Decision 894](https://staticfile.hsx.vn/Uploads/LocalFiles/a7cd428d1034455187af830f1cfc9d63/20210113_HOSE_QD%20sua%20doi%20quy%20dinh%20thoi%20gian%20giao%20dich%2C%20bien%20do%2C%20don%20vi.pdf) · [SSI trading and settlement rules](https://www.ssi.com.vn/en/individual-customer/transaction-regulation-on-hnx?page=1)

VinaCapital reported a 2m VND minimum purchase and redemption rates of 2%, 1.5% and 0.5% in April 2020 and January 2021. The May 2021 factsheet lists 2%, 0.5% and 0% with a 2m VND minimum. This study switches fee bands on 11 June 2021, the date in the May factsheet filename. Mirae Asset's 10 June 2022 guide specifies a 100k VND minimum and the lower rates. These observable dates approximate transitions across distributors. Fund NAV already includes its management expenses. The individual investor's sale tax is modeled at 0.1%. VFF traded on Tuesdays before its board resolution of 16 August 2021 to add Thursdays. The archived NAV has regular Thursdays from 9 September 2021, plus a single earlier Thursday on 6 May 2021. The simulation uses Tuesdays through August and both weekdays from September, conditional on an exact dated NAV. A new order can first be filed on the following session for the next eligible dealing day. A five-session expiry is a simulation convention. [Issuer amendment](https://vinacapital.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/20220511-vff-tom-tat-sua-doi-bo-sung-l-pl-xxviii-052022-final.pdf) · [2025 prospectus](../phase2_ocb_deposits/rebalance_5/evidence/vff-prospectus.pdf)

GOLD uses the evidence-prioritized SJC HCMC composite. Both directions and the eligible decision midpoint use the same units, VND per lượng. An unchanged ask or synthetic carry blocks that day's gold fill under the primary policy. Other orders can fill. The historical GOLD–K5 protocol suppressed the entire trading event in its separate experiment.
